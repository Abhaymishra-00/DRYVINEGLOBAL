What is Dryvine Global?

Dryvine Global is an import and export business focused on dry fruits, seeds, kernels and specialty nuts.

What products do you offer?

Our catalogue includes popular dry fruits, seeds, premium dry fruits and other dry fruits and nuts. Please visit the Products page for the full list.

Can I enquire about bulk orders?

Yes. Contact us with the products you need, approximate quantity, destination and any preferred specifications so we can discuss your enquiry.

Do you support import and export enquiries?

Yes. Dryvine Global is an import and export business. Share your destination market and requirements so details can be discussed.

How can I check availability and pricing?

Use the Contact Us page to request current availability, specifications, packaging details and a quotation. Pricing may depend on product, quantity and destination.
